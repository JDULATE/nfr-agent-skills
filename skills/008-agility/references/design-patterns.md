# Responding to a changed delivery priority

Use inspected delivery practices as constraints. The patterns below do not require
a particular methodology, cadence, role title, or process tool.

## Outcome slice under retained gates

- **Use when:** A changed priority is too broad to deliver within its requested
  response window. Identify a useful accepted result rather than just smaller tickets.
- **Trade-off:** A narrower slice may defer capabilities the requester values;
  obtain acceptance of that difference rather than redefining completion silently.
- **How to verify:** Trace the slice to the new priority's result and retained
  release gates; reject closure records that show only planning work.

## Interrupted-work record

- **Use when:** A new order pauses work already underway. Retain produced artifacts,
  unresolved decisions, obligations, owner, and the condition that permits restarting.
- **Trade-off:** Preserving work has cost; continuing it may block the new priority.
  Expose both consequences instead of treating cancellation or pausing as free.
- **How to verify:** Resume a representative paused item from the retained record
  and identify reconstruction work or missed obligations not shown in the revised plan.

## Dependency decision boundary

- **Use when:** Another owner or an unavailable input blocks the urgent slice.
  Show the wait, the accepted alternative scope, and who can choose that alternative.
- **Trade-off:** Parallel work can produce unusable partial results; reducing scope
  can weaken the priority outcome. Preserve those limitations in the plan comparison.
- **How to verify:** Rehearse a dependency wait and inspect whether elapsed response
  timing and displaced work stay visible when the plan changes again.

## Timing contract

Retain the priority decision event, accepted-plan event, delivery endpoint, working
calendar, waits, revised commitments, and decision authority. Distinguish time to
an accepted plan from time to an accepted delivered result.
An illustrative proposal is an accepted revised plan within 2 working days;
confirm with the user/stakeholder. Use the stakeholder's calendar rather than
inventing working hours or dropping inconvenient blocked intervals.
