# Essential dispatch during a site loss

This fictional stakeholder selects issuing acknowledged assignments to reachable
teams as essential. Historical analytics are nonessential. The severe scenario
removes the normal dispatch site, central store, and its identity/control services.

## Stated survivors and constraints

The candidate alternate has an approved local mission roster, independently usable
operator authority, a local work ledger, and an agreed channel to reachable teams.
These are assumed inputs to verify, not claims that an offline label supplies them.
Freshness, authority, and which assignments are permitted require stakeholder confirmation.

| Event/input | Required essential output | Carried state |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Site and central identity absent | Alternate starts with actual declared survivors | No borrowed central login |
| assignment-slate to reachable team-amber | Permitted assignment acknowledged through surviving channel | Stable identity and actual acknowledgement retained |
| Analytics unavailable | No false mission failure from this nonessential feature | Deferred function recorded |
| Proposed action exceeds degraded authority | No invented permission | Unavailable decision/owned gap recorded |
| Normal site returns | Reconcile assignment-slate before normal replay | No duplicate mission command assumed safe |

## Proposed horizon

Illustrative example; confirm with the user/stakeholder:
Preserve the agreed essential assignment result through 30 minutes of the held
site loss. Ask which duration, mission decisions, freshness, and authority apply;
that horizon cannot establish survival of other disruptions or unreachable teams.

## Negative trace and handback

If the alternate must contact central identity before issuing a permitted command,
its surviving-resource premise fails. If it only records a local intention without
team acknowledgement, it has not produced this example's essential result.
On handback, compare stable assignment identity and actual effects before replay.
A restored central service alone cannot show the mission continued during loss.
No site-loss exercise, recipient acknowledgement, or actual authority check occurred here.
