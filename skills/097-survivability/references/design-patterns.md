# Essential-mission practices

Let the stakeholder define the essential result and severe disruption boundary.

## Mission/result prioritization

- Use when normal resources cannot support all ordinary functions during disruption.
- Trade-off: deferring features preserves capacity or attention but can remove a
  result the mission actually requires; selecting convenient survivors is insufficient.
- Verify: map essential outputs to explicit stakeholder decisions and keep
  nonessential losses separate from failures of the minimum mission contract.

## Surviving-resource dependency closure

- Use when an alternate site, offline mode, or manual path is proposed.
- Trade-off: alternate paths require their own credentials, data, communication,
  authority, and operators; apparent separation can hide a lost shared prerequisite.
- Verify: remove the declared normal dependencies and inspect every mandatory
  alternate step with only the stated survivors. A standby process heartbeat is not a result.

## Degraded data and authority contract

- Use when fresh data or ordinary decision authority may be unavailable.
- Trade-off: degraded decisions may preserve essential work while introducing
  consequences the stakeholder has not permitted; urgency is not automatic authority.
- Verify: exercise actual remaining information, freshness limits, identity, and
  permission rules; leave unsupported decisions unavailable rather than inventing approval.

## Disruption work ledger

- Use when alternate work must later merge into normal records.
- Trade-off: stable identity and effect evidence require local bookkeeping; losing
  that ledger can make essential actions impossible to reconcile on return.
- Verify: retain accepted, deferred, conflicting, and unknown mission actions
  with their actual outcomes throughout the disruption, within the permitted data policy.

## Constrained handback

- Use when restored normal authority overlaps the alternate's work.
- Trade-off: reconciliation can delay normal resumption, while immediate merging
  can duplicate commands or overwrite the result the mission mode preserved.
- Verify: rehearse pending and conflicting actions, authority transfer, and actual
  effects before treating handback as complete; do not infer success from normal service health.
