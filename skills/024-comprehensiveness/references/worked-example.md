# Import-guide breadth example

This fictional guide currently explains starting an import. It does not yet
explain how readers decide what to do after a partial result.

## Inputs and carried questions

Synthetic job `import-slate` uses input `records-amber`. Example behavior accepts
valid rows and retains rejected-row reasons. Restart semantics remain unknown.
The audience includes the operator and the person receiving rejected data.

| Reader question | Needed subject | Current output | Proposed output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| How do I start? | Initiation and prerequisites | Start instructions | Retain source-checked discussion |
| Which data was accepted? | Result interpretation | Missing subject | Explain accepted/rejected result meanings |
| Who fixes rejected data? | Responsibility and handoff | Missing subject | Add stakeholder-confirmed handoff discussion |
| Can I restart safely? | Recovery and duplicates | Unknown behavior | Owned source/policy gap, not invented advice |

Carry the result and unresolved questions into the topic map; do not turn a
partial-result scenario into an unconditional restart recommendation.

## Requirement proposal

Illustrative discovery trial; confirm with the user/stakeholder:
Inspect 3 contrasting journeys: initiation, partial-result review, and correction
handoff. The trial scopes discovery, not a universal comprehensive-guide threshold.
For the agreed audience, provide usable discussions for the approved subject
areas and retain the unresolved restart topic as an explicit gap.

## Verification and rejection

Open the proposed result interpretation from the operator's guide location and
check that it explains how accepted and rejected outputs differ. A heading called
Recovery with no decision-supporting content does not fill the recovery domain.
A completed fixed field list would be a completeness check; it would not reveal
the absent handoff topic. No actual guide or audience feedback was evaluated here.
